PaisaVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

About this policy

This is PaisaVault’s privacy policy. It explains what stays on your phone, what optional Google services may receive, and how ads work on the free plan. Last updated 22 September 2026.

Who we are

PaisaVault is a personal finance app that runs on your Android device. We do not operate a PaisaVault account system or a cloud database of your spending.

Financial data on this phone

Transactions, budgets, bills, goals, SMS imports, and related money data are stored in an encrypted local database on your phone. PaisaVault does not upload merchants, amounts, SMS contents, or balances to our servers — we do not run those servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you are not on PaisaVault Pro, Google AdMob may show banner, native, app-open, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may use your Advertising ID, device and app identifiers, approximate location, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads. AdMob does not receive your transactions, SMS, or balances. Personalized ads in the EEA, UK, and similar regions run only after you respond to the in-app consent form.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on backup, PaisaVault writes an encrypted file to your own Google Drive app-data folder. Google provides that storage; PaisaVault does not keep a copy. You can disconnect Drive at any time in Backup settings.

Crash reports

Optional crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) may include device model, OS version, and stack traces so we can fix bugs. They are not meant to include financial data. You can leave crash reporting off if the app offers that choice.

Google Play Billing

If you buy PaisaVault Pro, Google Play Billing processes the purchase. Google receives the payment details needed to complete it. PaisaVault only learns whether your subscription is active. The plan renews until you cancel it from Google Play subscriptions.

Permissions

SMS access is optional and used only to import messages you choose for auto-categorizing transactions. Notification permission is used for bill and budget reminders. You can revoke either in Android settings.

Device screen lock

When you open PaisaVault, Android may ask you to confirm with the screen lock already set on this phone (fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password). Android performs that check. PaisaVault does not receive, store, or upload your fingerprint, face data, or device PIN, pattern, or password.

Children

PaisaVault is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Upgrade to Pro to remove ads, or cancel Pro from Google Play subscriptions. Use Manage ad preferences in the app to review or change AdMob consent. Turn off Google Drive backup, SMS auto-import, or uninstall the app to stop optional processing. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the on-device database from this phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date and the text of this policy. Continued use after an update means you accept the policy currently shown here and in the app.
